# Product v0 policy

Product v0 is the public, theory-driven implementation overlay for the V3 normative model. It uses a frozen subset of REPORT_CORE, explicit deterministic rules, and real participant answers. It is allowed to provide a complete local product while the research track continues separately.

`THEORY_DRIVEN_PROVISIONAL` is not `EMPIRICALLY_CALIBRATED`. Product v0 thresholds, item selection, weights, confidence limits, and public wording are implementation choices derived from the normative construct and combine semantics. They are auditable and replaceable, but they are not claims of psychometric validation.

The research P0/P1 forms remain research-only. They do not define the Product v0 route, session key, scoring, or report. Raw Product v0 answer events remain immutable so a future scoring version can derive a new profile without rewriting historical answers.

The product describes a person's current answers, preferences, capacity, and provisional behavior clues. It does not diagnose, rank people, calculate compatibility, predict relationship success, recommend partners, or infer a hard boundary from an ordinary preference. A hard boundary appears only when the user explicitly answers a hard-condition item using the authored hardness grammar.

The local vertical slice is the deliverable for this version. Cloud persistence and public-home cutover are intentionally deferred until the real-answer path has passed regression tests.
